# Home (single page)

Mode: Experience. The work leads; the interface recedes.

Audience: visitors arriving from analog.am, Mastodon/Pixelfed and Tigran's projects who want to see the work and find a way to see more or get in touch.

Constraints (user-pinned, 2026-10-19): an art website; texts, photos, videos and the visited-cities map mixed on ONE page; simple white page; big spaces between contents. Earlier answers: name + a few photos first; must not feel like a template or corporate; not too much motion. Keep the map and the gift wishlist. The Blog and Links pages are dropped.

## Direction contract

THESIS: one long white sheet hung like a small exhibition: each piece (text, photograph, film, map) placed alone, off-centre, with a lot of white around it. It refuses the even photo grid and the hero-plus-cards template.
OWN-WORLD: pure white ground, near-black ink, one grotesque (Hanken Grotesk, Noto Sans Armenian for Armenian) in two sizes plus small captions; no boxes, rules, cards or shadows; the photographs are the only colour; dark ink dots on a grey map.
STORY: the visitor meets the name and one sentence, then walks down through alternating pieces, understands photographer-first and also builder, and ends at a colophon with contacts, projects and wishlist.
FIRST VIEWPORT: name and one-line bio top-left at moderate size; one large photograph offset right, starting near the vertical middle and running below the fold so the page invites scrolling.
FORM: user-pinned brief (white exhibition sheet), overriding the roll; seed:7a3d46c49b442a3e.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
